SearchPoint 0.32.0 lets you find files from WhatsApp and introduces easy step-by-step guides to help you get started.

## Search Files from WhatsApp

Easily find documents, PDFs, and photos shared in your WhatsApp chats on Windows!

- **Find chat files in seconds**: Link your WhatsApp once in Settings, and SearchPoint will automatically find documents and attachments from your chats and groups, complete with who sent them.
- **Why it matters**: No more scrolling through endless chat messages just to find a receipt, agreement, or file someone sent you.
