SearchPoint 0.29.0 introduces AI renamer rule customisation.

## AI Renamer Rule Builder

Take full control of how your files are organized with customizable AI renaming conventions!

- **Custom Rules from Settings**: You can now design and fine-tune custom naming conventions for AI file renaming from Settings. Specify format, dates, and domain-specific tags that the AI renamer will apply across target directories.
- **Why it matters**: Keep all your project folders consistent and tidy without tedious manual renaming.
