SearchPoint 0.33.0 lets connected AI tools answer questions directly from your documents, makes organizing files effortless with custom tags, and brings search to your Microsoft OneNote notebooks.

## Ask What Documents Say with AI

Let your connected AI assistants answer questions directly from your files!

- **Instant document answers**: Connected AI tools (like Claude or Cursor) can now read and answer questions about facts inside your documents—such as who signed an agreement, key dates, or invoice totals—complete with quotes from the text.
- **Why it matters**: Get immediate answers to specific questions about your contracts and reports without having to open and read through every page yourself.

## Organize & Search with Tags

Group your important documents together and find them with a single click!

- **Tag your files**: Add custom tags to one or many files at once. You can quickly filter by any tag directly from the search bar or the home screen.
- **Why it matters**: Keep all documents for a project in one place without having to reorganize your folders or duplicate files.

## Search Inside OneNote Notebooks

Find notes and meeting minutes right alongside your other documents!

- **OneNote search built-in**: Click the new OneNote filter to instantly search through your notebook pages and sections saved in OneDrive or SharePoint.
- **Why it matters**: Stop jumping between different notebooks just to recall an idea, note, or meeting agenda.
