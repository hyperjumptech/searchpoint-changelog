SearchPoint 0.34.0 makes team collaboration seamless with an easier way to invite coworkers to share OneDrive files, plus instant sharing alerts.

## Invite Coworkers to Share Files

Help your team find documents faster by inviting colleagues to connect their OneDrive!

- **Invite in a few clicks**: Easily look up teammates by name or email right from SearchPoint to invite them to share their OneDrive access. Clicking an invitation opens SearchPoint directly to connect without extra steps.
- **Why it matters**: Break down silos and stop emailing files back and forth by making team documents searchable for everyone.

## Teammate File Sharing Alerts

Know the moment a coworker shares their files so you can collaborate right away!

- **Instant notifications**: When a teammate turns on file sharing for their OneDrive, you'll see a friendly alert with a quick button to connect and view shared files.
- **Why it matters**: Work together smoothly without needing to ask colleagues if they've shared project folders with you.
