SearchPoint 0.31.0 extends your search across the apps you use every day, with SearchPoint inside StartGPT and new connectors for Outlook and Sticky Notes.

## SearchPoint inside StartGPT

Search everything without leaving your workflow — SearchPoint is now embedded directly inside StartGPT!

- **Built-in access**: Run SearchPoint queries from within the StartGPT interface, using the same on-device index you already know.
- **Why it matters**: No more switching apps or breaking focus just to find a file, email, or note.

## Connect Outlook

Bring your Outlook world into SearchPoint and search your mailbox alongside your files.

- **Connect from Settings**: Link your Outlook account to index messages, attachments, and calendar items into your unified search results.
- **Why it matters**: Find that email attachment or meeting detail in seconds instead of scrolling through your inbox.

## Connect Sticky Notes

Your quick notes are no longer a black hole — SearchPoint can now search your Sticky Notes.

- **One-click connection**: Enable the Sticky Notes connector from Settings to index the content of your notes automatically.
- **Why it matters**: Stop losing track of ideas, phone numbers, and reminders jotted down in a hurry.
